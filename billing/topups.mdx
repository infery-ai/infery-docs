Need more credits than your plan covers? Buy **topups** — one-off packs that sit on top of your monthly allowance and **never expire**.

## Buying

**Billing** → **Top up** → pick a preset, or type your own amount.

The presets buy **1 000**, **2 000**, **5 000**, **10 000** or **20 000**
credits. A custom amount can buy anything from **100** to **100 000**.

What that costs is not listed here — Billing shows it in the currency your
workspace is charged in, and checkout shows the exact amount before you pay.

## Payment

Stripe-powered — any major card (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), Apple/Google Pay on mobile. Card-only, same as plan billing — no SEPA or other bank-debit method. Instant credit delivery on successful payment.

Invoice is emailed to the billing contact immediately.

## Burn order

When you call the API, we deduct:

1. **Monthly credits first** (don't waste what will expire on non-rollover plans)
2. **Topup credits second** (they never expire — use them last)

You see the split in Billing → **Balance**.

## Refunds

An unused balance is refundable to the card you paid with, minus the payment-processor fee. Credits you have already spent cannot be refunded, and neither can credits that expired at the end of a cycle. UK and EU consumers have 14 days from a purchase to cancel and be refunded in full.

There is no self-service refund — open a support ticket, or email [support@infery.ai](mailto:support@infery.ai). The full policy is [Section 9 of the terms](https://infery.ai/terms#refunds).

## Auto-topup

Enable in Billing to auto-charge when balance drops below a threshold you set. Useful for production workloads that must never pause.
