## Plans at a glance

| Plan | Price | Monthly credits | Team | Workflows at once | API keys | Projects | History | Storage | Rate limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | $0 | 50 trial only | 1 | 1 | 2 | 5 | 7 days | 1 GB | 20 rpm |
| **Creator** | $19 | 1 200 | 3 | 3 | 5 | 25 | 30 days | 5 GB | 60 rpm |
| **Studio** | $49 | 3 500 | 5 | 5 | 15 | 75 | 90 days | 10 GB | 150 rpm |
| **Team** | $99 | 8 000 | 10 | 10 | 40 | 200 | 360 days | 50 GB | 350 rpm |
| **Enterprise** | Contact | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Custom | 1 000 rpm |

Scheduling is not capped by count on any plan — what a tier buys is how OFTEN a
schedule may fire, in the table further down.

Free is pay-as-you-go: the full catalog, the playground and the API are all
available, and every run is charged against your balance. The monthly credits on a
paid plan are a grant, not a cap — spend past them and usage continues at list
price, topped up manually or by auto-recharge.

**Unused monthly credits roll over, up to three months' worth.** On a paid plan
the unspent part of an allowance carries into the next cycle; anything above
three times your monthly allowance expires at renewal. Free grants no monthly
credits, so it banks none, and topped-up credits never expire either way. Your
credit history records both what was carried and what expired.

**Storage is a threshold, not a wall.** The figure above is what the plan
includes; anything beyond it is billed separately at **10 credits per GB-month**,
charged daily against your balance. Uploads are refused only if that overage goes
unpaid — see [Storage usage](/billing/usage).

## Paying by the year

Creator, Studio and Team can be paid annually at **ten months for twelve** —
$190, $490 and $990 a year. The discount is on the **fee**, not on usage: the
monthly credit allowance and the Studio spend discount are identical on both
periods, because usage carries a margin that has no room to give and the platform
fee does.

<Note>
Annual checkout is not switched on yet. The plan rows and Stripe prices exist;
what is missing is the monthly credit grant for an annual subscriber, since a
year is one Stripe invoice and the credits have to arrive twelve times.
</Note>

## Studio spend discount

Paid plans spend credits further **in the Studio** — the dashboard, the
playground and workflows you run from them:

| Plan | Discount on Studio spend |
| --- | --- |
| **Creator** | 5% |
| **Studio** | 8% |
| **Team** | 10% |
| **Enterprise** | negotiated |

It applies to what a run **costs**, not to the credits a plan grants: credits are
granted at par and go further when spent from the Studio. The charge you see on a
Studio run is already discounted, and the credit-history row records the
percentage that was applied.

**It never applies to the API.** A call authenticated with an API key is always
charged list price, on every plan. Requests are told apart by which credential
they carry, so the discount cannot be obtained by calling the API from a browser.

## Feature flags

Some features are only available on higher plans:

- `streamingEnabled` — streaming SSE responses (every plan)
- `apiAccess` — API keys available (every plan, Free included)
- `customQuotas` — presets and per-key limits (Creator+)
- `fallbackChains` — auto-fallback on errors (Studio+)
- `priorityQueue` — faster routing to providers (Team+)
- `require2faForPrivilegedRoles` — 2FA mandatory for admins (Team+)
- `scheduledPipelines` — [run a workflow on a schedule](/workflows/schedules) (Creator+)
- `dedicatedSupport` — Slack Connect with our team (Enterprise only)
- `sso` + `sla` — Enterprise only

Scheduling also carries a floor on how often a schedule may fire, and the floor is
what the higher plans buy:

| Plan | Shortest interval |
| --- | --- |
| **Creator** | every hour |
| **Studio** | every 15 minutes |
| **Team** | every 5 minutes |
| **Enterprise** | every minute |

## Switching plans

Settings → Billing → **Change plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately (prorated). Downgrades take effect at next billing cycle.
